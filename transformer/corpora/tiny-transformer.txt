the sun was warm and the river was quiet.
the little fish swam through the blue water.
a small bird sat on a branch above the river.
hello, said the fish. where are you going today?
i am going to the sea, said the bird.
the fish and the bird followed the river together.
they passed green trees and smooth stones.
the water sparkled in the morning light.
when the sun went down, the friends stopped to rest.
the stars shone above the quiet river.
tomorrow will bring a new adventure, said the bird.
the little fish smiled and dreamed of the sea.

the next morning, a gentle rain fell on the forest.
the bird sheltered beneath a broad green leaf.
the fish watched tiny circles dance on the water.
rain helps the trees grow, said the bird.
and the river carries the water to the sea, said the fish.
after the rain, the sky became clear and blue.
the friends followed a rainbow past a field of flowers.
a turtle was resting on a warm flat stone.
take your time, said the turtle. the sea will still be there.
the fish and the bird laughed and slowed down.
there was so much to see along the way.
