the little fish swam through the blue water.
the sun was warm and the river was quiet.
a small bird sat on a branch above the water.
the fish looked up and said hello to the bird.
hello little fish, said the bird. where will you go today?
i will follow the river and find the sea, said the fish.
the bird spread its wings and flew beside the river.
together they passed green trees and smooth stones.
when the sun went down, the friends stopped to rest.
the stars shone above the quiet water.
tomorrow will bring a new adventure, said the bird.
the little fish smiled and dreamed of the sea.
